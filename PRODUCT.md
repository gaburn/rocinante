# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers coordinating many local Copilot and Claude coding sessions across repositories.
They need to understand current agent work, group it into workstreams, and intervene without
switching among terminal windows.

## Product Purpose

Rocinante is a real-time dashboard for organizing, monitoring, and interacting with agentic
coding sessions. Success means users can quickly see what is running, blocked, waiting, or
complete and move directly to the session that needs attention.

## Brand Personality

Practical, direct, dependable.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative SaaS dashboards, unfamiliar controls, excessive motion, and visual effects
that compete with operational state.

## Design Principles

- Keep current agent state visible and trustworthy.
- Reuse familiar dashboard and developer-tool interactions.
- Reveal detail progressively without hiding required actions.
- Prefer dense, scannable information over decoration.
- Make intervention paths obvious and reversible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard operation, clear focus states, semantic controls, and
reduced-motion support.
